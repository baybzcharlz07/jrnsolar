Let’s talk about your roof
A few things you might be wondering

Good questions. Clear answers.

Will solar eliminate my electricity bill?

Not necessarily. Solar can offset part of your electricity use, but your bill depends on system size, daytime consumption, weather, utility charges, and any export arrangement. A tailored assessment helps set realistic expectations.

Will the panels work during a power outage?

A standard grid-tied system normally shuts down when the grid goes down. Backup power requires a compatible system, usually with batteries and the right isolation and backup equipment. Discuss the appliances you want to keep running with JRN.

How much does installation cost?

The price depends on your energy needs, equipment, roof conditions, and whether you include batteries. Share your monthly bill and location to start a discussion about a property-specific quote.

Is my roof suitable for solar panels?

Available space, shade, roof direction, condition, and structural suitability all matter. These need to be reviewed before the final installation plan is agreed.

What should I prepare for my inquiry?

A recent electricity bill, your property location, and a few roof photos are useful. You can start with the form above or call 0966 805 3179. Ask JRN to confirm service availability in your area.
